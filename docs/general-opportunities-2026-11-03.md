## How to use the records

Most of the official research pages still describe completed 2026 application
rounds. Use them to prepare, not as evidence that a new round is open. No 2027
deadline was generated by shifting an old date forward. Programme dates and
application dates are kept separate.

Each record includes official verification sources dated November 3, 2026,
eligibility, location, fee uncertainty and funding qualifications. Paid research
placements and internship allowances are not tuition scholarships. Funding
figures apply to the reviewed call and must be rechecked for later cohorts.

ICPC rules were read in a rendered browser. MeitY's current guidelines and
schedule were checked against the official portal. UNV is a service with
individual assignments, rather than one centrally dated recruitment round.

The targeted audit checked 18 new source URLs: 15 returned HTTP 200, and the
three MeitY URLs returned HTTP 403 automated-access warnings. Their official
contents were independently retrieved during source review. No hard failures
were reported; this does not certify future availability or application status.

No ordinary courses, new scholarship records, or duplicates of the existing
CERN summer/openlab programmes, GSoC, LFX, Outreachy or MLH were added. The
Technical Studentship is a separate CERN programme with a longer placement
and different eligibility.
