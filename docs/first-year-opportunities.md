# Opportunities for first-year undergraduates

Reviewed November 3, 2026. Start by searching **first-year** in the
[directory](https://amoghcode.github.io/student-opportunities/). This batch adds
three programme records to the existing 35. Academic-year eligibility does not mean that
applications are currently open or that every first-year student qualifies.

| Opportunity | First-year fit | What to do next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Aspire Leaders Program](../opportunities/aspire-leaders-program.yml) | First-year students can qualify, but applicants must also meet age and first-generation or limited-income requirements. | 2026 applications are closed; the official page offers 2027 interest notifications. |
| [Summer of Bitcoin](../opportunities/summer-of-bitcoin.yml) | No minimum university year in the student guide; technical experience and full-time availability are required. | Build Git and project experience while waiting for an announced application cycle. |
| [Jane Street FTTP](../opportunities/jane-street-fttp.yml) | Specifically for first-year STEM undergraduates; nearest-session geography rules apply. | Sign up for official notifications. No current India-specific session was confirmed. |

## Important distinctions

- **First-year** means your academic year. **First-generation** means your
  family's university-education background; these are separate conditions.
- Technical eligibility does not mean beginner readiness. Summer of Bitcoin
  expects programming or design and open-source experience.
- An interest form is not an open application, and an announced programme
  schedule is not necessarily its application window.

## Research decisions

Scholarships are excluded from this expansion to keep the repository focused
on programmes, internships, fellowships and practical experiences. The existing
35 records are unchanged.

Microsoft Explore was not added for this India-focused first-year batch:
[its official FAQ](https://careers.microsoft.com/v2/global/en/exploremicrosoft)
currently lists India for the second-year application, while its first-year
application lists the United States, Canada and Mexico. A generic description
of Explore as being for first- and second-year students is insufficient.

Established research programmes that require two completed university years
were not repackaged as first-year opportunities. Existing records such as
Google Summer of Code and Millennium Fellowship were not duplicated.

Each new YAML record contains the official sources, access date, restrictions,
and unresolved timing details. Fees without an explicit statement remain
unknown. The website's open-status labels still depend on recorded dates;
consult the notes and official page when a deadline is unannounced.
